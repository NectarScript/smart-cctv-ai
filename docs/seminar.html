Introduction
Introduction
Problem

Traditional CCTV systems only record video. Detecting suspicious activity depends on human operators watching many feeds at once.

Human attention degrades sharply after 20 minutes of continuous monitoring. Critical events are missed, and intelligent commercial solutions remain proprietary and expensive.

Our Approach
1

Multi-Camera Ingestion

Live capture from webcams, IP cameras, and video files

2

Person Detection

YOLOv8 + ByteTrack for identity tracking

3

Behaviour Analysis

Pose-based rules for suspicious actions

4

Web Dashboard

Real-time streaming with alerts

Literature Survey
Literature Survey
Video Ingestion

2000OpenCV VideoCapture

Bradski. Standard camera interfacing API used across surveillance and vision systems.

2016RTSP Protocol

RFC 7826. Dominant IP camera streaming standard for real-time video transport.

2019Drop-oldest Buffering

Chen et al. Real-time surveillance practice — favours recency over completeness.

Detection & Tracking

2023YOLOv8 (Ultralytics)

Jocher et al. Modern one-stage object detector; strong CPU inference at nano size.

2022ByteTrack

Zhang et al. ECCV 2022. Reduces ID switches vs SORT by associating every box.

2017DeepSORT

Wojke et al. Appearance-based tracking; higher accuracy, higher compute cost.

Behaviour Analysis

2020MediaPipe Pose

Bazarevsky et al. BlazePose 33-keypoint model, on-device pose estimation.

2018ST-GCN

Yan et al. AAAI. Graph CNN on skeletons for action recognition — dataset heavy.

—Rule-based Systems

Deployed in BriefCam, Avigilon. Interpretable but low generalisation to new events.

Aim and Objective
Aim and Objective
Aim
To design and implement an intelligent multi-camera surveillance pipeline that detects and classifies suspicious human behaviour in real time using open-source AI components.

Deliver a web dashboard with live streaming and event log.

Module 1
Multi-Camera Ingestion & AI Vision
Purpose

Acquire raw video from heterogeneous sources — laptop webcams, IP cameras over RTSP, and pre-recorded video files — and deliver clean, timestamped frames to the downstream AI pipeline without blocking.

Responsibilities

Manage the lifecycle of N camera streams

Threaded capture to avoid blocking the AI loop

Bounded queue with drop-oldest policy

Frame preprocessing (crop, colour, resize)

Limitations & Next Steps
Current Limitations & Next Steps
Current Limitations
—

No frame synchronisation across cameras

—

No auto-reconnect for RTSP on connection loss

—

CPU-only decoding (no hardware acceleration)

—

Frame-drop rate not exposed as a metric

—

No cross-camera identity association

Planned Next Steps
▸

RTSP auto-reconnect with exponential backoff

▸

Expose frame-drop rate on web dashboard

▸

Optional GPU-accelerated decoding (NVDEC)

▸

Frame-timestamp synchronisation across cameras

▸

Adaptive frame-rate based on pipeline load
